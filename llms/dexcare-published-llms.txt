# DexCare: The navigation platform patient access run on

> DexCare is infrastructure for patient navigation and scheduling to grow patient volume without adding clinical resources\. The platform connects data across EMRs, workflows, and access points so health systems route patients to available care, regardless of where they enter or what modality they need\. The problem? Capacity exists but systems can't see it\. Results: 40% more patients treated and 5\-day reduction in wait times\. DexCare serves 57M\+ patients across 50 states, including Providence, Kaiser Permanente, and Piedmont\.
